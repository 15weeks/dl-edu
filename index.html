Deep learning, step by step in pictures

Fifteen study pages on deep learning, written so a first-year university student can follow them. Every chapter runs the same way: a question → the terms you need → the idea → a worked example by hand → a figure you can move yourself → one line to remember. Chapters 1–13 and the GAN part of chapter 15 follow the lecture list of a Udemy deep-learning course; the Transformer chapter (14) and the diffusion part of chapter 15 are written new. Click 한국어 at the top of the left menu for the Korean version.

§The 15-chapter plan

Click a chapter name to see what it covers, its goal, its sections and its interactive figures.

§How to use these pages

To do thisDo this
Open a pageEnglish pages have plain names (index.html, ch01-intro.html); Korean pages end in -KR.html. Double-click to open in Chrome. Formulas and figures work offline; only the font falls back to the system font.
Switch languageThe 한국어 / English button at the top of the left menu opens the same page in the other language.
Move between chapters and sectionsUse the left menu. The current chapter's sections are listed, and the section you are reading is highlighted. The ☰ button at the top left folds the menu away and remembers that choice.
Go to other pagesThe "Other pages" list at the bottom of the left menu. Add one line to DLEDU_LINKS in assets/dl.js and it appears in every chapter.
Edit the plan or add a chapterChapter names, topics, sections and figure plans all live in assets/plan.js. For a new chapter, create chNN-name.html (English), chNN-name-KR.html (Korean) and chNN.js (figures, shared by both), then set ready: true in plan.js. Run node tools/plan_to_md.js to regenerate PLAN_KR.md and PLAN_EN.md.
Check the example numbersRun tools/ch01_numbers.py and tools/ch01_sims.py to recompute every number on the page with NumPy, SciPy and PyTorch.
